Why Choose Us?

At Xo Boba, we believe that a great drink is an experience, not just a refreshment. We take pride in our diverse menu, which caters to boba veterans and newcomers alike, while staying mindful of our guests' needs through our dedicated allergens guide. Whether you are stopping by for the "Drink of the Day" or relaxing with friends in our pink-hued sanctuary, we strive to make every cup a highlight of your day. We are more than just a cafe; we are a local destination for flavor, creativity, and curating the perfect cup of boba tea.

Our Story

Vanny's “Bobarista" Journey started in a background pool party, his discovery of making drinks brought his family and friends together, he saw firsthand how a great beverage could turn a simple hangout into a lasting memory. This encouraged him to refine his boba-making abilities and sparked the idea of creating drinks with a professional eye. An inspiration curated by the traditional boba culture of his roots in Asia, he set out to develop a menu that caters to both boba veterans and newcomers alike.
